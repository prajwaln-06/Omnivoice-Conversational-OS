[FEVER_CLUSTER]
Symptoms: high temperature, chills, shivering, body ache, sweating.
Guidance: Check if temperature is above 100F. Suggest Paracetamol/Dolo 650. Rest and hydration.

[RESPIRATORY_CLUSTER]
Symptoms: cough, sore throat, runny nose, sneezing, chest congestion.
Guidance: Suggest steam inhalation. Check for breathing difficulty or wheezing.

[GASTRO_CLUSTER]
Symptoms: stomach pain, acidity, bloating, nausea, loose motion.
Guidance: Recommend ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts) and light food like curd rice. Avoid spicy meals.
